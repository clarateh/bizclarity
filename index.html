What I cover关注领域

Four pillars every owner should check每个老板都该检查的四大领域

For Sdn Bhd companies, LLPs, partnerships and sole proprietorships. Tap a pillar to see what we look at.适用于私人有限公司、有限责任合伙（LLP）、合伙企业与独资企业。点击任一领域，看看我们会检查什么。

Control: who really decides?控制权：公司到底谁说了算？

What we look at我们会检查

  • Your shareholders' or partnership agreement (or the lack of one): deadlock, reserved matters, pre-emption, drag / tag-along股东协议或合伙人协议（或者根本没有）：僵局机制、保留事项、优先购买权、领售 / 随售
  • Board composition: who appoints and removes directors董事会组成：谁能委任、撤换董事
  • Whether your constitution and your agreement actually match公司章程和协议是否互相配合
  • Partnerships and LLPs: how decisions are made, and what happens when a partner leaves, falls out or passes away合伙企业与有限责任合伙（LLP）：怎么做决定、合伙人退出、闹翻或离世时怎么办
  • What happens to the shares if you, or a fellow shareholder, go through a divorce如果你或其他股东离婚，股份会怎样

Worth checking if如果你有这些情况，值得检查

  • Ownership is split equally: 50-50, or three or more equal partners股权平分：50-50，或三位以上合伙人平均持股
  • It's a family business, and nothing is in writing家族企业，但什么都没写下来
  • An investor or silent partner has come in有投资人或隐名合伙人加入
  • The business has grown or changed, but the agreement hasn't been updated生意已经成长或改变，协议却一直没更新

Continuity: what if you're not there tomorrow?营运持续性：如果明天你不在了？

What we look at我们会检查

  • Who can sign, run the bank accounts and pay salaries if you're incapacitated or pass away你失去能力或离世时，谁能签字、操作银行户口、发薪水
  • Deputies and delegated authority: who can step in, and with what powers代理人与授权：谁能接手、有什么权限
  • Key-person dependency, and how the lost profits would be replaced while you find a successor, e.g. key-person cover对关键人物的依赖，以及在找到接替者之前，利润损失如何弥补，例如关键人物保障

Worth checking if如果你有这些情况，值得检查

  • You're the only director, or the only bank signatory你是唯一的董事或唯一的银行签署人
  • The business would stall without one person少了某一个人，生意就会停摆
  • Key clients, banks or suppliers deal only with you主要客户、银行或供应商只跟你一个人打交道

Handover / Exit: leaving on your terms接班 / 退出：按你的方式离开

What we look at我们会检查

  • Handing over the running of the business to family or professional management: timeline, roles and who decides what把经营权交给家人或专业经理人：时间表、角色、谁决定什么
  • Passing your shares on, using the legal structure that suits your intentions用符合你心意的法律架构，把股份传下去
  • Buy-sell agreement: who buys a partner's shares if they leave, fall ill or pass away, at what price, and how it's funded股份买卖协议：合伙人退出、生病或离世时，谁来买、什么价钱、资金从哪里来
  • Readiness for a sale: valuation, records, due diligence and the warranties you'd give出售前的准备：估值、记录、尽职调查和你要给的保证
  • Walking away clean: releasing your personal guarantees, and restrictions such as non-competes干净地离开：解除个人担保，以及竞业限制等条款

Worth checking if如果你有这些情况，值得检查

  • You'd like to step back in the next 3-5 years你打算在 3 到 5 年内退下来
  • A buyer or investor has approached you有买家或投资人来接触你
  • Your children may or may not want to take over孩子不一定想接班
  • You have partners but no buy-sell agreement你有合伙人，但没有股份买卖协议
  • Your will doesn't mention your company shares, or names one executor遗嘱没提到公司股份，或只写了一个执行人

Asset ringfencing: protect what your family relies on, now and for the next generation个人资产隔离：守住家人依靠的，现在和下一代

What we look at我们会检查

  • Every personal guarantee you've signed: amount, cap, scope, joint and several, continuing你签过的每一份个人担保：金额、上限、范围、是否连带、是否持续有效
  • Which assets sit in your name and which in the business's, and whether they're properly ringfenced哪些资产在你名下、哪些在公司名下，有没有做好隔离
  • Sole proprietors and partners: unlimited personal liability, and whether to move to a company or LLP独资与合伙经营者：个人承担无限责任，以及是否该转为公司或 LLP
  • Wills, trusts, nominations, foundations and family office structures, set up early and for genuine reasons遗嘱、信托、受益人指定、基金会与家族办公室架构：要趁早，而且要有合理的理由

Worth checking if如果你有这些情况，值得检查

  • You've signed guarantees for company loans or trade credit你为公司贷款或供应商赊账签过担保
  • Your family depends on your income家人依靠你的收入
  • You want to preserve your wealth across generations你希望财富能跨代传承
  • Most of your wealth is reinvested in, or tied to, your company你大部分的财富都投回或绑在公司里，没有分散

Blind spots owners tell me they never saw coming老板们都说"没想到"的盲点
#01

The company owes RM3m. How much do you pay?公司欠300万，你要还多少？

A joint and several personal guarantee can put the whole debt on you, not your share of it.连带个人担保，可能让你一个人背全部的债，而不只是你的那一份。

#02

No shareholders' agreement. Partners fight. Company wound up?没有股东协议，吵到公司被清盘？

Without a deadlock and exit mechanism, a 50-50 fight can end in court.没有僵局和退出机制，50对50的争执可能一路闹上法庭。

#03

You won the case. Why can't you get paid?官司赢了，却拿不回钱？

A judgment is only paper if the debtor has nothing left to collect.如果对方已经没有资产，判决书只是一张纸。

#04

Shares sold. The bank still wants RM2m?股份套现了，银行还追你200万？

Selling out doesn't end your personal guarantee unless the bank releases you.卖掉股份，不等于担保结束——除非银行正式解除你的担保。

#05

Will written. Shares end up with a stranger?写了遗嘱，股份却落在陌生人手上？

Through the chain of executorship, your child's executor can end up running your company.通过执行人链，孩子的执行人可能最后接管你的公司。

#06

Guarantee enforced. Moving the house to family won't help.担保被追，房子转给家人也保不住

Gifts made in the years before bankruptcy can be clawed back by the Department of Insolvency.破产前几年内白送的资产，破产管理署可以追回。
